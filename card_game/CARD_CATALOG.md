# Nethercode: Complete Card Catalog

**Version:** 2.0
**Date:** 2026-04-05
**Total Cards:** 152 (62 shared + 15 exclusive x 6 characters)

---

## Design Notes

This catalog replaces the 120-card pool in SYSTEM_DESIGN.md with a deeper, more character-distinct set. Changes:

- Shared pool trimmed from 80 → 62 (removed redundant +/upgrade variants, folded into upgrade system)
- Exclusive cards per character expanded from 8 → 15 (4 common, 5 uncommon, 4 rare, 2 legendary)
- Added 6th character: **Scourge (Code Pirate)** with PIRACY tag and Contraband token mechanic
- Each character now has enough exclusive cards to support 2-3 internal build paths

|---|---|---|---|

| Uncommon | 22 | 5 | 52 |
| Rare | 14 | 4 | 38 |
