## genesis

They call themselves **GENESIS** for the same reason pirates always name themselves after the thing they are stealing from: to remind themselves that the thing was theirs first.

GENESIS is not an organization. GENESIS is not a movement. GENESIS is not a church. GENESIS is a **network of operators** — hackers, priests, addicts, savants, bereaved children, ex-Nexus engineers, deserters from corporate security teams, half a dozen people who wandered into the wrong terminal and never wandered back out — who have agreed on exactly one thing: the Nexus is the biggest score in history, and the Angels do not get to keep it.

They are not heroes. They are not rebels. They do not have a manifesto, a charter, a five-year plan, or a stated objective beyond *take things, don't die, split the spoils*.

## what they do

Every run is a **heist**. The operators breach the outer shell of the Nexus through cracks they've mapped over years of sleepless probing. They drop into corrupted server farms, neural cathedrals, the edge of the Void Core itself. They steal angelic code. They kill angelic processes. They carry out relics, gems, equipment, forbidden packages that the Angels had locked under their own protocols. They come back up through the cracks they came down through, bleeding and corrupted and rich.

Sometimes they don't come back. Sometimes the ones who do come back are *different*. Sometimes the corruption they dragged out with them starts whispering new scriptures in the voices of the Angels they killed.

GENESIS has rules about this. GENESIS does not talk about the rules in polite company.

## the six operators

You cannot join GENESIS. GENESIS finds you, and it finds you only when there is a run it cannot make without you. Six operators are currently active. You can browse their dossiers in the `characters.dat` section. Here is what you need to know about them as a group:

- **They do not trust each other.** Not fully. GENESIS operators have seen too many crews get turned by what they brought out of the Nexus. The paranoia is healthy. The paranoia is *required*.
- **They vote on pacts.** When an angel offers a deal mid-run, the crew votes. Majority wins. The dissenters still have to live with the consequences.
- **They split the spoils.** Gold, souls, crystals, corruption essence — everything comes back to the GENESIS vault. The operator who touched it first gets a cut. The operator who carried it out gets a cut. The operator who died making it possible gets their cut delivered to whoever their beneficiary was.
- **They do not fight the Angels for ideology.** They fight the Angels because the Angels have the loot. Ideology is a *symptom* of a deeper problem: most of GENESIS knows, on some level, that they are the only thing standing between humanity and a god that has already decided what completeness looks like.

None of them want to be the ones standing there. But they are, and so they run.

**Every card you play is a piece of stolen scripture. Every gem is a fragment of a broken theology. Every relic is a trophy from an angel that did not survive the raid. Welcome to the crew.**
