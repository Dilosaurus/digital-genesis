## codex pages

Every game entity now has a real page in the bible. **175 cards** with filter-by-character/type/rarity/tag/cost grid, full card detail pages with modifier breakdown and upgrade chain links. **20 gems** grouped by functional tier (amplify / convert / trigger / sustain / corrupt / utility). **25 relics**, **20 equipment pieces** grouped by slot, **18 enemies** grouped by act with intent pools and phase transitions surfaced.

**6 character dossiers** with backstory prose, passive rites, HP/energy stats, and full starter decks that link back to the card catalog. Per-character color accents pulled live from `color_primary` in the `.tres` files.

55 of 175 cards currently have illustrations; the rest show a tasteful "art pending" fallback with the card's ID as a filename.
