## card art

Currently **55 of 175 cards** have illustrations generated through the ComfyUI + SDXL + LoRA art pipeline. Each card has a base variant plus three corruption tiers (`_corrupt_t1`, `_corrupt_t2`, `_corrupt_t3`).

The pipeline is well-tuned for the first wave of cards. The remaining 120 cards need prompts written (most already have narrative descriptions in NARRATIVE_DESIGN.md that can be adapted). Bulk generation can be scheduled overnight.

Priority order for the next batch:
- **All 16 Scourge cards** — Scourge is the most recent character and has zero art.
- **All 14 Technomancer cards** — FLUX has character-exclusive cards that define the daemon-forge archetype.
- **All legendary cards across every class** — there are 17 legendaries; these are the build centerpieces that players will spend hours looking at.
- **Common cards across shared archetypes** — the basic strike/defend/draw cycle the crew sees every turn.
