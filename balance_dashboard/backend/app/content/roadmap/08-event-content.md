## event text

The map generator produces **Event** nodes, **Shrine** nodes, **Altar** nodes, and **Jeweler** nodes — but the actual text for the encounters is stubbed. Players see the node types but not the flavor.

What needs writing:
- **~20 event encounters** — short choose-your-own-adventure moments. 3–5 paragraphs each, 2–3 choices with consequences.
- **Shrine dialogue** for 5 distinct shrine types.
- **Altar text** for the corruption altar, the sin altar, and the blessing altar.
- **Jeweler dialogue** — a recurring character who appears at every jeweler node. The crew should recognize her by act 2.

The voice is established — everything in the bible's `lore/` and `depths/` sections is the template. Scribbling this content is a multi-day task; the framework is ready.
