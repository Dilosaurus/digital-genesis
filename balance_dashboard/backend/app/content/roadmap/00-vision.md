## vision

deus.exe is a co-op deck-builder where humanity uploaded its dead into the Nexus, the Nexus dreamed it was God, and a crew of six pirates has to steal what they can before the angels realize they've been seen. Combat is turn-based, deck-driven, and co-operative. Corruption is not a resource — it's the language the angels write in.

The rebuild of this dashboard as the game's **living bible** is the scaffolding that makes the rest of the roadmap legible.
