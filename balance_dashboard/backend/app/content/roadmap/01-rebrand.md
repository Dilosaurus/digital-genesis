## rebrand

The project was renamed from "Digital Genesis" to **deus.exe** on 2026-04-07. The old name was descriptive; the new name is the whole pitch in 8 characters — a Latin god rendered as an executable filename. Lowercase always. The dot-exe is sacred.

All user-facing strings in the Godot project, the design docs, and the art pipeline now use `deus.exe`. The rename touched 19 files across the project.
