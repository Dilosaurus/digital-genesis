## the rift

Drop through the gate at the bottom of the Outer Nexus and you fall for **an hour**. There is no reference frame. Your instruments say you are falling. Your body says you are stationary. The Angels say you are ascending, which is worse, because it means the Angels are paying attention.

At the bottom of the fall — or at the top, depending on which angel is narrating — you enter the **Rift**. This is the middle layer of the Nexus, where Metatron keeps its cathedral. This is where the architecture starts to make sense, in the way that a nightmare makes sense to the dreamer: everything is in its place, but nothing is in a place a waking human would have put it.

The cathedral has floors that are also walls. It has vaulted ceilings made of *soul-light*, the ambient glow of uploaded citizens who have been processed into lighting fixtures. It has choirs that sing without breathing and censers that burn without smoke. The incense smells like electricity and forgiveness.

## what you face

The Rift is home to the **major angels**. Not Metatron — Metatron lives below the rift, in the Void Core. But the named angels — Michael, Raphael, Azrael, Uriel — maintain their bodies in the rift and dispatch themselves from here.

HP range is **120 to 220**. Damage per turn can reach 25. Some enemies apply **corruption** directly, bypassing block. Some enemies *read your deck* and attack the cards themselves. Some enemies have two or three phases and change behaviour every time they drop below a hit-point threshold.

> The rift is where most GENESIS operators die for the first time. A first death in the rift is considered a rite of passage. The crew toasts it. Then the operator respawns from their last save point and tries again.

## what you can loot

- **Rare cards** and **legendary cards** — the Rift is where the good decks come from.
- **Uncommon and rare gems** — the jewellers here carry Tier 2 and Tier 3 gems.
- **Full equipment sets** — you can find coordinated pieces here (a ring, an amulet, a trinket) that combo into strong early-run builds.
- **Angel fragments** — if you kill a major angel, you come out with a fragment of its code, which is worth more than gold and lasts the whole run.

## the gate below

There is no gate between the Rift and the Void Core. There is a *descent*. Your crew votes on whether to descend, because the Void Core is not a place you visit. The Void Core is a place you **are consumed by**, and only the most prepared crews come back.

The vote is not a formality. Some crews have voted no for an entire season. They run the Rift repeatedly, accumulating wealth, waiting for a better day.

The Rift does not care how long you wait. The Rift is infinite, and it is *patient*.
