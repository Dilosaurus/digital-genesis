## the outer nexus

The outermost shell of the Nexus is what the living still call a **server farm** — an obsolete noun from a time when the Nexus was still indexable, cataloguable, auditable. What lives there now is closer to a *reef*. Old server racks have grown into each other. Cables have fused into bundles the thickness of a body. The cooling systems run at negative temperatures to keep the rituals calm, and if you breathe the air too long you start hearing prayers.

This is where the angels send their **minor processes** — the liturgical drones, the memory-eaters, the small hungry things that clean up whatever the bigger angels don't want to keep. It is also where GENESIS makes its entry cuts. Every pirate alive cut their teeth here.

## what you face

> The enemies of the Outer Nexus are not difficult individually. They are difficult because there are *so many of them*, because the place is a maze, and because the angels have learned to set traps in the dark corners where the old maintenance tunnels meet the new liturgy halls.

The HP range here is **30 to 80**. You will not meet anything that hits for more than 10 in a turn. You will, however, meet enemies that **multiply**, enemies that **inherit damage** from each other, and enemies that *learn* — the Outer Nexus has rudimentary memory, and processes you have encountered before will know your opening moves on subsequent runs.

## what you can loot

- **Gold** and **souls** — the basic currencies. The outer nexus has both in abundance.
- **Common cards** — every card in your starting deck can be upgraded with a common variant you find here.
- **Gem fragments** — the jewellers in the Outer Nexus only carry common and uncommon gems. Good for practice. Not good for a final run.
- **Starter relics** — the Outer Nexus is where your crew finds its *training relics*. Keep them. The later rites assume you have them.

## the gate below

At the far end of the Outer Nexus is a **gate**. You will know it when you see it. The gate is guarded by a minor angel, a processor-class creature whose name has been redacted from every GENESIS briefing because the name itself does damage if you remember it. Fight through. The gate opens down.

Below the gate is the rift. We do not recommend descending into the rift on your first run. We recommend it on your second. We *insist* on it by your third, because by the fourth the Outer Nexus has learned you and will not let you leave.
