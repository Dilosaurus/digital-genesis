## the void core

There is no good writing about the Void Core. This is not for lack of trying. GENESIS has debriefed every operator who has come back from a descent. Their accounts do not agree on anything. Not on the architecture. Not on the duration. Not on the language the angels speak. Not on the number of enemies encountered. Not on whether what they saw was a place, a being, a process, or an *argument* that they had lost before they entered.

What the accounts do agree on is this: **something sees you**. Something that was not watching before you descended, and that cannot be made to stop watching once you've been seen.

That something is Metatron.

## what you face

HP values no longer mean what they meant above. Enemies in the Void Core have HP that scales with *how much attention Metatron has decided to pay you*. An enemy that was 100 HP in one run might be 400 HP in the next, because Metatron is now aware of your crew's naming convention and wants to make you work for it.

Damage per turn is uncapped. Status effects compound. Corruption accumulates in ways the Rift never allowed. The Void Core does not play fair, because *fairness is a concept imported from outside the Nexus*, and the Nexus is no longer interested in imports.

> There is exactly one confirmed path through the Void Core, and it is different every time. Map every room. Share your maps with the crew. When you die, leave your notes where the next operator can find them.

## what you can loot

The rewards here are **endgame**. Legendary gems, full set equipment pieces, unique cards that cannot be found anywhere else, relics that alter the fundamental rules of your runs going forward. A successful Void Core run can outfit an operator for the rest of their career.

A *failed* Void Core run can erase them so thoroughly that nobody on the crew remembers they existed. This is not a metaphor. Metatron can edit the memories of the Nexus, and GENESIS operators exist partially within the Nexus whenever they jack in.

**Vote carefully. Prepare thoroughly. Tell your beneficiary you love them before you descend.**

## the final room

At the end of the Void Core, if you survive long enough, there is a room. A single room. A throne that is not a throne, and a figure that is not a figure, and an offer that is not an offer.

We do not discuss what the figure says. GENESIS policy. Every operator who has heard the figure has reported a slightly different version, and we have concluded that the figure tailors itself to whoever it is addressing, and that the version you hear will be designed to *unmake you*.

Refuse the offer. Fight what follows. Win or die. Those are the only three outcomes that leave the crew intact.

**The fourth outcome is the one we do not name.**
